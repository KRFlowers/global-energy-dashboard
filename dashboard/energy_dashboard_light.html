Dashboard story & notes

Electricity is decarbonising meaningfully — renewable share has grown from ~19% to ~32% globally since 2000. Within electricity, replacing coal delivers ~2× the climate impact of replacing gas.

But electricity is only ~20% of total energy. The remaining 80% — industrial heat, transport, heating, agriculture — has barely transitioned. The IPCC's 2023 synthesis report (AR6) identifies these hard-to-electrify sectors as the current priority bottleneck for meeting climate targets.

The data here shows the easy half of the problem. The harder half remains.

Two-part takeaway:

  1. Replace high-carbon fuels first — coal-first prioritisation within electricity.
  2. Address total energy, not just electricity — sector breadth beyond the power grid.
